## ✨ The Experience

Hover around the capsule to draw out dynamic spikes, then click to compress the entire liquid-metal button. Four spike styles let you shift between soft, organic, sharp, and wild surfaces.

## 🧩 Add it to Framer

1. Open your Framer project.
2. Create a **Code Component**.
3. Copy the contents of [`KarimSaifFerrofluidSpikeButton.tsx`](./KarimSaifFerrofluidSpikeButton.tsx) into it.
4. Place the component on your canvas.
5. Adjust the property controls and connect the **Click Action** to your desired interaction.

The source is designed for the Framer code-component environment; it imports `addPropertyControls`, `ControlType`, and `useIsStaticRenderer` from `framer`.

## 🎛️ Customize Your Own Ferrofluid

**Appearance** — background, liquid color, metallic highlights, accent, text color, typography, dimensions, gloss, reflection, and shadow.

**Spikes** — count, width, length, preset, magnetic attraction radius, and strength.

**Motion** — spring, damping, wave speed, autoplay, autoplay delay/speed, pause, and press scale.

**Cursor & particles** — custom cursor toggle, style, color, size, particle visibility, and particle limit.

## ♿ Accessibility & Performance

The component uses an HTML button for native keyboard activation and Framer click actions. It includes a static state for Framer rendering, checks `prefers-reduced-motion`, and uses visibility and intersection observers to avoid unnecessary offscreen activity.

**Note:** This repository contains the component source as supplied for the project. Behavior should be tested in Framer Preview and the published website, especially at extreme sizes and settings.
